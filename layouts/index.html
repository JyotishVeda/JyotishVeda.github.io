WHERE JYOTISH STARTS

A circle of 360°, divided two ways.

Indian astrology measures from the fixed stars, not from the equinox. Subtract the ayanamsha from a tropical longitude and you have the sidereal longitude every calculation on this site is built on.

12 rashis of 30°

360° ÷ 12 = 30° — the rashi is found by floor(longitude ÷ 30). Your janma rashi is the rashi of the Moon, not the Sun.

Explore the 12 rashis →

27 nakshatras of 13°20′

360° ÷ 27 = 13°20′, and each nakshatra divides again into four padas of 3°20′. The Moon's nakshatra fixes the whole Vimshottari dasha sequence.
