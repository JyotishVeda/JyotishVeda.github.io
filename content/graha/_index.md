*Graha* is usually translated as "planet", but it means something closer to *that which
seizes*. The nine grahas are the seven visible moving bodies plus the two lunar nodes, Rahu
and Ketu, which are mathematical points rather than objects.

Two things are worth noticing immediately. There is no Uranus, Neptune or Pluto in the
classical system — everything in it was visible to the unaided eye. And the Sun and Moon are
counted as grahas alongside the planets, with the Moon carrying more weight than either in
most readings.

Each graha is read through four questions: which rashi it occupies, which bhava that rashi
falls in, what dignity it holds there, and what aspects reach it. The Vimshottari dasha then
decides *when* each graha delivers its results — the nine periods add to exactly 120 years.
