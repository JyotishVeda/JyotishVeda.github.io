## What Ketu signifies

Ketu — South Lunar Node — is graha number 9 in the standard ordering. Its
karakatva, the set of things it signifies, is: Detachment, moksha, past skill, sudden loss, occult insight.

The south node — detachment, inherited skill and release.

Rahu and Ketu are not physical bodies. They are the two points where the Moon's orbital plane crosses the ecliptic, always exactly 180° apart, and always moving backwards through the zodiac. Because they are mathematical points rather than objects, they own no rashi and act through whichever graha rules the sign they occupy.

## Dignity

| Condition | Placement |
| --- | --- |
| Own sign | No sign rulership (acts through its dispositor) |
| Moolatrikona | Not assigned |
| Exaltation | Commonly Vrishchika or Dhanu, by school |
| Debilitation | Commonly Vrishabha or Mithuna, by school |

Dignity is the first thing a reader checks. A graha in its own sign or exaltation delivers
its significations readily; the same graha debilitated delivers them with friction, delay or
distortion. Neither is a verdict on its own — a debilitated graha with a strong dispositor,
or one whose debilitation is cancelled, behaves very differently from the textbook case.

## Relationships

Ketu counts Mangal, Shukra, Shani as natural friends and Surya, Chandra as
enemies, with Budha, Guru neutral. These
relationships drive the Graha Maitri koota in kundali matching, where the two rashi lords are
compared and up to five of the thirty-six points are awarded on the strength of that
friendship.

## Aspects

Ketu casts 5th, 7th and 9th houses (school-dependent). Every graha aspects the 7th house from itself; Mars,
Jupiter and Saturn have additional special aspects, and the nodes are given the Jupiter-like
5th, 7th and 9th by many schools. An aspect is read as Ketu extending its nature onto
the aspected bhava and its occupants.

## Timing: the 7-year period

In the Vimshottari system Ketu holds a mahadasha of **7 years** out of
the 120-year cycle. The dasha opens with whichever graha rules the Moon's birth nakshatra —
for Ketu that means Ashwini, Magha, Mula. During its own period a graha delivers the
results indicated by its house position, sign, dignity and aspects; outside that period it
works mostly through transit.

## Traditional associations and remedies

| Association | Ketu |
| --- | --- |
| Weekday | Tuesday (with Mangal) |
| Direction | North-west |
| Gemstone | Cat's eye (Lehsunia) |
| Metal | Mixed alloys |
| Deity | Ganesha |
| Beeja mantra | *Om Sram Sreem Sraum Sah Ketave Namah* |
| Nature | Shadowy malefic, subtracts and detaches |

Remedial measures in the tradition run from mantra recitation and fasting on the graha's
weekday to charity associated with its significations. Gemstones are the most commonly sold
remedy and the one requiring most caution: a stone strengthens a graha, and strengthening a
graha that rules a difficult bhava for your particular lagna can work against you. Nothing on
this page should be treated as a prescription, and none of it replaces medical, legal or
financial advice.
