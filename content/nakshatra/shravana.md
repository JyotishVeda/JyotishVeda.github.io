## Position in the circle

Shravana is nakshatra number 22 of 27, occupying 10°00′ – 23°20′ Makara. Every nakshatra is
exactly 13°20′ wide, because 360° ÷ 27 = 13°20′. Divide that again by four and you get the
four padas of 3°20′ each, which is how a nakshatra is subdivided for the navamsha and for
name-syllable selection.

Its starting sidereal longitude is 280.0°. If the Moon's sidereal longitude at birth
falls between 280.0° and 293.3333°, Shravana is the janma
nakshatra.

## Lord, deity and symbol

**Lord: Chandra (Moon).** This is the single most consequential fact on the page. The nakshatra
lord of the Moon decides which mahadasha a person is born into, and the fraction of the
nakshatra already crossed decides how much of that period is left. A birth in Shravana
means the Vimshottari sequence opens with Chandra.

**Deity: Vishnu.** The presiding deity is not decoration — in the classical method the
deity is the interpretive key. Vishnu tells you what the nakshatra is *for*.

**Symbol: Ear, three footprints.** The symbol gives the concrete image the tradition thinks with:
the hearing — knowledge received by listening.

## Classification

| Category | Shravana | What it is used for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gana | Deva | Temperament matching in Guna Milan (6 of 36 points) |
| Yoni | Monkey (female) | Instinctive compatibility (4 of 36 points) |
| Nadi | Antya | The most heavily weighted koota (8 of 36 points) |
| Varna | Mleccha | Traditional social-function grouping (1 point) |
| Guna | Rajas | The quality of activity the nakshatra expresses |
| Direction | North | Used in muhurta and vastu applications |

A Deva gana with Antya nadi is the combination that will show up when this
nakshatra is used in kundali matching. Nadi carries eight points on its own, and an identical
nadi on both sides scores zero, so Shravana pairs poorly on that one koota with every other
Antya-nadi nakshatra regardless of how well the rest of the chart fits.

## Themes

The keyword cluster for Shravana is listening, learning, scripture, reputation. Work traditionally
associated with the nakshatra: teaching, linguistics, audio and media, law, counselling, scriptural study.

These associations come from the deity and symbol rather than from any statistical study.
They are worth reading as a description of a *mode of action* — the way this part of the
sky is said to get things done — rather than as a career recommendation.

## Where it sits among the rashis

Shravana falls in Makara (Capricorn), the prithvi (earth) rashi ruled by
Shani (Saturn). That means two rulerships operate at once: Chandra as nakshatra
lord and Shani as rashi lord. When the two are natural friends the
nakshatra expresses smoothly; when they are enemies the tradition expects internal tension in
whatever the Moon signifies for that person.

## Padas

The four padas of Shravana each span 3°20′ and map onto the navamsha chart, which is
examined for marriage and for the deeper strength of every graha. Two people born under this
nakshatra in different padas can have substantially different navamsha charts. The
[Nakshatra Finder](/tools/nakshatra-finder/) returns the exact pada along with the
traditional name syllable.
