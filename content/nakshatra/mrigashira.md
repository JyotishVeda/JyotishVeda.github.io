## Position in the circle

Mrigashira is nakshatra number 5 of 27, occupying 23°20′ Vrishabha – 6°40′ Mithuna. Every nakshatra is
exactly 13°20′ wide, because 360° ÷ 27 = 13°20′. Divide that again by four and you get the
four padas of 3°20′ each, which is how a nakshatra is subdivided for the navamsha and for
name-syllable selection.

Its starting sidereal longitude is 53.3333°. If the Moon's sidereal longitude at birth
falls between 53.3333° and 66.6666°, Mrigashira is the janma
nakshatra.

## Lord, deity and symbol

**Lord: Mangal (Mars).** This is the single most consequential fact on the page. The nakshatra
lord of the Moon decides which mahadasha a person is born into, and the fraction of the
nakshatra already crossed decides how much of that period is left. A birth in Mrigashira
means the Vimshottari sequence opens with Mangal.

**Deity: Soma (Chandra).** The presiding deity is not decoration — in the classical method the
deity is the interpretive key. Soma (Chandra) tells you what the nakshatra is *for*.

**Symbol: Deer's head.** The symbol gives the concrete image the tradition thinks with:
the searching deer — perpetual seeking and light-footed curiosity.

## Classification

| Category | Mrigashira | What it is used for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gana | Deva | Temperament matching in Guna Milan (6 of 36 points) |
| Yoni | Serpent (female) | Instinctive compatibility (4 of 36 points) |
| Nadi | Madhya | The most heavily weighted koota (8 of 36 points) |
| Varna | Shudra | Traditional social-function grouping (1 point) |
| Guna | Tamas | The quality of activity the nakshatra expresses |
| Direction | South | Used in muhurta and vastu applications |

A Deva gana with Madhya nadi is the combination that will show up when this
nakshatra is used in kundali matching. Nadi carries eight points on its own, and an identical
nadi on both sides scores zero, so Mrigashira pairs poorly on that one koota with every other
Madhya-nadi nakshatra regardless of how well the rest of the chart fits.

## Themes

The keyword cluster for Mrigashira is searching, curiosity, sensitivity, wandering. Work traditionally
associated with the nakshatra: research, travel writing, surveying, perfumery, textiles, real estate scouting.

These associations come from the deity and symbol rather than from any statistical study.
They are worth reading as a description of a *mode of action* — the way this part of the
sky is said to get things done — rather than as a career recommendation.

## Where it sits among the rashis

Mrigashira falls in Vrishabha (Taurus), the prithvi (earth) rashi ruled by
Shukra (Venus). That means two rulerships operate at once: Mangal as nakshatra
lord and Shukra as rashi lord. When the two are natural friends the
nakshatra expresses smoothly; when they are enemies the tradition expects internal tension in
whatever the Moon signifies for that person.

## Padas

The four padas of Mrigashira each span 3°20′ and map onto the navamsha chart, which is
examined for marriage and for the deeper strength of every graha. Two people born under this
nakshatra in different padas can have substantially different navamsha charts. The
[Nakshatra Finder](/tools/nakshatra-finder/) returns the exact pada along with the
traditional name syllable.
