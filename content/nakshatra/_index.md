The nakshatra system divides the same 360° circle into 27 parts instead of 12:
`360° ÷ 27 = 13°20′`. Each nakshatra divides again into four **padas** of `3°20′`, giving 108
padas in total — the same 108 that recurs throughout Indian tradition.

Nakshatras are older than rashis in Indian astronomy and carry more interpretive weight in
practice. The Moon's nakshatra at birth decides three things at once:

- the **janma nakshatra**, used for muhurta, matching and personal ritual
- the opening **mahadasha** of the Vimshottari cycle, through the nakshatra's ruling graha
- the traditional **name syllable**, taken from the pada

In kundali matching, three of the eight kootas — Tara, Yoni and Nadi, worth 15 of 36 points —
are read entirely from the nakshatra. The [Nakshatra Finder](/tools/nakshatra-finder/)
returns yours along with the pada and all of its classifications.
