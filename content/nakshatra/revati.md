## Position in the circle

Revati is nakshatra number 27 of 27, occupying 16°40′ – 30°00′ Meena. Every nakshatra is
exactly 13°20′ wide, because 360° ÷ 27 = 13°20′. Divide that again by four and you get the
four padas of 3°20′ each, which is how a nakshatra is subdivided for the navamsha and for
name-syllable selection.

Its starting sidereal longitude is 346.6667°. If the Moon's sidereal longitude at birth
falls between 346.6667° and 360.0°, Revati is the janma
nakshatra.

## Lord, deity and symbol

**Lord: Budha (Mercury).** This is the single most consequential fact on the page. The nakshatra
lord of the Moon decides which mahadasha a person is born into, and the fraction of the
nakshatra already crossed decides how much of that period is left. A birth in Revati
means the Vimshottari sequence opens with Budha.

**Deity: Pushan.** The presiding deity is not decoration — in the classical method the
deity is the interpretive key. Pushan tells you what the nakshatra is *for*.

**Symbol: Fish, drum.** The symbol gives the concrete image the tradition thinks with:
the wealthy — final nakshatra, protector of journeys and endings.

## Classification

| Category | Revati | What it is used for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gana | Deva | Temperament matching in Guna Milan (6 of 36 points) |
| Yoni | Elephant (female) | Instinctive compatibility (4 of 36 points) |
| Nadi | Antya | The most heavily weighted koota (8 of 36 points) |
| Varna | Shudra | Traditional social-function grouping (1 point) |
| Guna | Sattva | The quality of activity the nakshatra expresses |
| Direction | East | Used in muhurta and vastu applications |

A Deva gana with Antya nadi is the combination that will show up when this
nakshatra is used in kundali matching. Nadi carries eight points on its own, and an identical
nadi on both sides scores zero, so Revati pairs poorly on that one koota with every other
Antya-nadi nakshatra regardless of how well the rest of the chart fits.

## Themes

The keyword cluster for Revati is safe passage, completion, kindness, endings. Work traditionally
associated with the nakshatra: travel and logistics, animal care, childcare, music, spiritual guidance.

These associations come from the deity and symbol rather than from any statistical study.
They are worth reading as a description of a *mode of action* — the way this part of the
sky is said to get things done — rather than as a career recommendation.

## Where it sits among the rashis

Revati falls in Meena (Pisces), the jala (water) rashi ruled by
Guru (Jupiter). That means two rulerships operate at once: Budha as nakshatra
lord and Guru as rashi lord. When the two are natural friends the
nakshatra expresses smoothly; when they are enemies the tradition expects internal tension in
whatever the Moon signifies for that person.

## Padas

The four padas of Revati each span 3°20′ and map onto the navamsha chart, which is
examined for marriage and for the deeper strength of every graha. Two people born under this
nakshatra in different padas can have substantially different navamsha charts. The
[Nakshatra Finder](/tools/nakshatra-finder/) returns the exact pada along with the
traditional name syllable.
