## What Vrishchika actually is

Vrishchika is the stretch of the sidereal zodiac from 210° – 240°, counted from the fixed
star reference point rather than from the spring equinox. That difference — the ayanamsha —
is currently a little under 24°, which is why a person called Scorpio by a Western
magazine is often a different rashi in Jyotish. The rashi that matters most in Indian
astrology is the one holding the **Moon** at birth, not the Sun.

Being the 8th rashi makes Vrishchika a jala-element,
sthira-mode sign ruled by Mangal (Mars). Those three facts —
element, mode and lord — carry most of the interpretive weight. Fixed water sign of Mars — depth, secrecy and sudden change.

## The three structural facts

**Tattva: Jala (Water).** The element describes the medium the sign works in.
Water signs work through absorption and retention. They register what happened and keep it, which is both their depth and their difficulty.

**Swabhava: Sthira (Fixed).** Fixed signs hold things. They are associated with accumulation, persistence and resistance to being moved; the classical complaint is that they hold on past the point of usefulness.

**Lord: Mangal (Mars).** A rashi is read through its ruler. Wherever Mangal (Mars) sits in a chart,
and whatever condition it is in, is where the affairs of Vrishchika are actually decided.
Mangal signifies courage, siblings, land, energy, surgery, blood and conflict. It is exalted in Makara 28° and
debilitated in Karka 28°, and runs a 7-year Vimshottari period.

## Traits the tradition associates with Vrishchika

Classical texts describe the Vrishchika native through the sign's emblem — scorpion —
and through its jala element. The recurring descriptions are
intense, secretive, researching, resilient and transformative.

Read this carefully: these are descriptions of one factor in a chart, not of a person. A
Vrishchika Moon in the 6th bhava with an afflicted Mangal (Mars) looks nothing like a
Vrishchika Moon in the 5th with Mangal (Mars) exalted. The rashi supplies the flavour; the
bhava, the lord's condition and the running dasha supply the substance.

## The nakshatras inside Vrishchika

Vrishchika contains Vishakha (pada 4), Anuradha, Jyeshtha. Because the nakshatra system divides the same circle
into 27 rather than 12, most rashis share a nakshatra with a neighbour. Where inside these
210° – 240° the Moon falls determines the janma nakshatra, the pada, and — crucially — which
graha's mahadasha the person is born into.

## Kalapurusha and the body

In the Kalapurusha scheme the twelve rashis map onto a single cosmic body from head to foot.
Vrishchika, as rashi number 8, corresponds to **pelvis and reproductive organs**. In
medical Jyotish this correspondence is read alongside the 6th bhava and the condition of the
rashi lord; it is a traditional framework and not a substitute for a doctor.

## Traditional associations

The day associated with Vrishchika is Tuesday, the day of its lord. The colour
group is deep red, maroon, the number is 9, and the stone
traditionally prescribed for strengthening Mangal (Mars) is Red coral (Moonga).

The beeja mantra for the rashi lord is *Om Angarakaya Namah*, traditionally recited on
Tuesdays. Gemstone prescription in particular should not be done from a rashi page —
a stone strengthens a graha, and strengthening a graha that rules a difficult bhava for your
lagna is the opposite of helpful.
