## What Kanya actually is

Kanya is the stretch of the sidereal zodiac from 150° – 180°, counted from the fixed
star reference point rather than from the spring equinox. That difference — the ayanamsha —
is currently a little under 24°, which is why a person called Virgo by a Western
magazine is often a different rashi in Jyotish. The rashi that matters most in Indian
astrology is the one holding the **Moon** at birth, not the Sun.

Being the 6th rashi makes Kanya a prithvi-element,
dwisvabhava-mode sign ruled by Budha (Mercury). Those three facts —
element, mode and lord — carry most of the interpretive weight. Dual earth sign of Mercury — discrimination, craft and correction.

## The three structural facts

**Tattva: Prithvi (Earth).** The element describes the medium the sign works in.
Earth signs work in durable, material terms — what can be held, kept, measured or built on. Their weakness is inertia rather than haste.

**Swabhava: Dwisvabhava (Dual).** Dual signs adapt and distribute. They sit between the fixed and movable phases, and are associated with mediation, teaching, transition and doing two things at once.

**Lord: Budha (Mercury).** A rashi is read through its ruler. Wherever Budha (Mercury) sits in a chart,
and whatever condition it is in, is where the affairs of Kanya are actually decided.
Budha signifies intellect, speech, trade, calculation, skin and nervous system. It is exalted in Kanya 15° and
debilitated in Meena 15°, and runs a 17-year Vimshottari period.

## Traits the tradition associates with Kanya

Classical texts describe the Kanya native through the sign's emblem — maiden holding grain in a boat —
and through its prithvi element. The recurring descriptions are
analytical, exacting, service-minded, anxious and skilled.

Read this carefully: these are descriptions of one factor in a chart, not of a person. A
Kanya Moon in the 6th bhava with an afflicted Budha (Mercury) looks nothing like a
Kanya Moon in the 5th with Budha (Mercury) exalted. The rashi supplies the flavour; the
bhava, the lord's condition and the running dasha supply the substance.

## The nakshatras inside Kanya

Kanya contains Uttara Phalguni (padas 2–4), Hasta, Chitra (padas 1–2). Because the nakshatra system divides the same circle
into 27 rather than 12, most rashis share a nakshatra with a neighbour. Where inside these
150° – 180° the Moon falls determines the janma nakshatra, the pada, and — crucially — which
graha's mahadasha the person is born into.

## Kalapurusha and the body

In the Kalapurusha scheme the twelve rashis map onto a single cosmic body from head to foot.
Kanya, as rashi number 6, corresponds to **abdomen and intestines**. In
medical Jyotish this correspondence is read alongside the 6th bhava and the condition of the
rashi lord; it is a traditional framework and not a substitute for a doctor.

## Traditional associations

The day associated with Kanya is Wednesday, the day of its lord. The colour
group is green, earth tones, the number is 5, and the stone
traditionally prescribed for strengthening Budha (Mercury) is Emerald (Panna).

The beeja mantra for the rashi lord is *Om Budhaya Namah*, traditionally recited on
Wednesdays. Gemstone prescription in particular should not be done from a rashi page —
a stone strengthens a graha, and strengthening a graha that rules a difficult bhava for your
lagna is the opposite of helpful.
