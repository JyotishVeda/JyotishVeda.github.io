## What Mesha actually is

Mesha is the stretch of the sidereal zodiac from 0° – 30°, counted from the fixed
star reference point rather than from the spring equinox. That difference — the ayanamsha —
is currently a little over 24°, which is why a person called Aries by a Western
magazine is often a different rashi in Jyotish. The rashi that matters most in Indian
astrology is the one holding the **Moon** at birth, not the Sun.

Being the 1st rashi makes Mesha a agni-element,
chara-mode sign ruled by Mangal (Mars). Those three facts —
element, mode and lord — carry most of the interpretive weight. Fiery, movable sign of Mars — beginnings, courage and raw initiative.

## The three structural facts

**Tattva: Agni (Fire).** The element describes the medium the sign works in.
Fire signs act before they deliberate, generate their own momentum and lose interest once the thing is started rather than finished.

**Swabhava: Chara (Movable).** Movable signs begin things. They are associated with travel, change of position and the initiating phase of any cycle; the classical complaint about them is that they rarely stay for the ending.

**Lord: Mangal (Mars).** A rashi is read through its ruler. Wherever Mangal (Mars) sits in a chart,
and whatever condition it is in, is where the affairs of Mesha are actually decided.
Mangal signifies courage, siblings, land, energy, surgery, blood and conflict. It is exalted in Makara 28° and
debilitated in Karka 28°, and runs a 7-year Vimshottari period.

## Traits the tradition associates with Mesha

Classical texts describe the Mesha native through the sign's emblem — ram —
and through its agni element. The recurring descriptions are
initiating, impatient, courageous, blunt and self-driven.

Read this carefully: these are descriptions of one factor in a chart, not of a person. A
Mesha Moon in the 6th bhava with an afflicted Mangal (Mars) looks nothing like a
Mesha Moon in the 5th with Mangal (Mars) exalted. The rashi supplies the flavour; the
bhava, the lord's condition and the running dasha supply the substance.

## The nakshatras inside Mesha

Mesha contains Ashwini, Bharani, Krittika (pada 1). Because the nakshatra system divides the same circle
into 27 rather than 12, most rashis share a nakshatra with a neighbour. Where inside these
0° – 30° the Moon falls determines the janma nakshatra, the pada, and — crucially — which
graha's mahadasha the person is born into.

## Kalapurusha and the body

In the Kalapurusha scheme the twelve rashis map onto a single cosmic body from head to foot.
Mesha, as rashi number 1, corresponds to **head and brain**. In
medical Jyotish this correspondence is read alongside the 6th bhava and the condition of the
rashi lord; it is a traditional framework and not a substitute for a doctor.

## Traditional associations

The day associated with Mesha is Tuesday, the day of its lord. The colour
group is red, scarlet, the number is 9, and the stone
traditionally prescribed for strengthening Mangal (Mars) is Red coral (Moonga).

The beeja mantra for the rashi lord is *Om Angarakaya Namah*, traditionally recited on
Tuesdays. Gemstone prescription in particular should not be done from a rashi page —
a stone strengthens a graha, and strengthening a graha that rules a difficult bhava for your
lagna is the opposite of helpful.
