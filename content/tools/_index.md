Every calculator here runs entirely inside your browser using an astronomical ephemeris and
the Lahiri ayanamsha. Birth details are never uploaded, logged or sold. By default they're
remembered on this device between tools, purely so you don't have to retype them — each form
has a checkbox to turn that off and a button to forget what's saved immediately.

Each tool shows the numbers it worked from, not just the verdict, so you can check the result
against any other ephemeris or software.
