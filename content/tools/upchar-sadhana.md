## How this page decides what to recommend

Most remedy pages work backwards from a single fact — "Saturn is in your 8th house" — straight
to a prescription. That skips the question classical remedial astrology actually asks first:
**what does this graha rule *from your lagna*?**

A graha that rules an angle (kendra: 1, 4, 7, 10) or a trine (trikona: 1, 5, 9) is a
**functional benefic** for your chart — worth strengthening, especially if it is currently
weak. A graha that rules only the difficult houses (6, 8, 12) is a **functional malefic** —
classical guidance is to *pacify* it through charity, mantra and lifestyle, not to strengthen
it with a gemstone. A graha ruling both kinds gets a balanced approach. Rahu and Ketu never
rule signs at all, so they are always approached through pacification, scaled to how afflicted
they currently are.

This page computes that classification from your actual chart, then layers on dignity (own
sign, exalted, debilitated, friendly or enemy sign), house placement, and retrograde status,
before recommending anything.

## What you get

- **Priority order** — which grahas your specific chart calls for the most attention on right
  now, and which need only light maintenance
- **Dasha-specific guidance** — the mahadasha lord's remedies matter most while that period
  runs; the antardasha lord's matter as a secondary, lighter layer
- **Per graha:** deity, mantra, japa count, day, yantra and direction; gemstone and substitute
  (or a clear note that a gemstone is not advised, when the graha is a functional malefic);
  colors to favour and to avoid; things to add to daily life; things to avoid; charity (daan);
  the traditional fasting day

## Read this honestly

Remedial astrology is a living cultural and devotional practice, not a mechanism with proven
causal effect. Nothing here should be read as a guarantee, and nothing here replaces medical,
legal or financial advice, or professional mental health support.

A few points worth taking seriously:

- **Gemstones are the remedy most likely to cause harm if mismatched.** Several — blue
  sapphire, hessonite and cat's eye especially — are known in the tradition itself to act
  unpredictably. Trial any stone on the skin for several days before committing, and never
  wear the gemstone of a graha this page marks as a functional malefic for your chart.
- **Be wary of anyone using a dosha or a "weak planet" to sell an expensive remedy.**
  Charity, mantra and lifestyle change cost little or nothing and are the remedies most
  consistently recommended across classical sources.
- **A remedy is a practice, not a transaction.** The traditional view is that sustained,
  sincere practice matters more than the specific object — the mantra recited for months
  outweighs the gemstone bought once.
