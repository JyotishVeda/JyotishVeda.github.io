## The eight kootas

| Koota | Points | Compares |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Varna | 1 | Traditional social-function grouping of the two rashis |
| Vashya | 2 | Which grouping each rashi belongs to |
| Tara | 3 | Nakshatra distance in both directions |
| Yoni | 4 | The animal symbol of each nakshatra |
| Graha Maitri | 5 | Natural friendship between the two rashi lords |
| Gana | 6 | Temperament: Deva, Manushya or Rakshasa |
| Bhakoot | 7 | Distance between the two rashis |
| Nadi | 8 | Ayurvedic constitutional grouping |
| **Total** | **36** | |

Eighteen is the conventional minimum. Twenty-four and above is usually described as a good
match, and above thirty as excellent.

## What a score does not tell you

Ashtakoota compares two Moons. It says nothing about the lagna, nothing about the 7th bhava or
its lord, nothing about Mangal dosha, nothing about the dasha periods either person is running,
and nothing about longevity indicators. A traditional astrologer treats 36 points as a first
filter, not a conclusion — and many pairs with high scores are rejected on other grounds, while
low-scoring pairs are cleared once cancellations are examined.

## Nadi dosha and its exemptions

Nadi carries the largest single weight, and an identical nadi on both sides scores zero out of
eight. Classical sources list several exemptions, including: both people having the same rashi
but different nakshatras, the same nakshatra but different padas, or the rashi lords of the two
charts being the same graha. Whether an exemption applies is a judgement call, which is exactly
why a calculator cannot close the question.

## Honest note on the tables

The Vashya and Yoni kootas are simplified here and the simplification is printed next to the
score. Dhanu and Makara are split between two vashya groups in the classical texts; this tool
assigns each rashi to one group. The full 14×14 yoni compatibility table varies between sources,
so this tool preserves the seven sworn-enemy pairs and grades the rest by temperament class.
Different software will therefore differ by a point or two on these two kootas.
