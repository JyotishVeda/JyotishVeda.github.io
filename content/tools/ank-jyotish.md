## The three numbers

**Moolank** (root number) is your birth day reduced to a single digit. Born on the 23rd:
2 + 3 = 5.

**Bhagyank** (destiny number) is the entire date of birth reduced the same way — every digit
of the day, month and year added together and reduced.

**Namank** (name number) uses the Chaldean letter values, which differ from the Pythagorean
table common in Western numerology. Chaldean assigns values 1 to 8 only; the number 9 is
treated as sacred and is not assigned to any letter.

## The graha mapping

Each digit is governed by a graha, which is what connects Ank Jyotish to the rest of Jyotish:

| Number | Graha | Number | Graha |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Surya | 6 | Shukra |
| 2 | Chandra | 7 | Ketu |
| 3 | Guru | 8 | Shani |
| 4 | Rahu | 9 | Mangal |
| 5 | Budha | | |

Whether Moolank and Bhagyank are "friendly" follows the natural friendships of their ruling
grahas — the same table used for Graha Maitri in kundali matching.

## Where this sits

Ank Jyotish is a traditional system with wide popular use, and it is the least astronomical
thing on this site: nothing here is derived from the sky. Read it as cultural practice and as
entertainment, and give it correspondingly less weight than a calculated chart.
