## The arithmetic

Your janma nakshatra is decided entirely by the Moon's sidereal longitude at birth. Divide
that longitude by 13°20′ and the whole number gives the nakshatra; divide the remainder by
3°20′ and you have the pada. The tool prints the longitude it used so you can check it.

## Why the pada matters

The pada fixes your position in the **navamsha** — the ninth-part divisional chart examined
for marriage and for the underlying strength of every graha. Two people born a few hours apart
in the same nakshatra but different padas can have materially different navamsha charts.

The pada also supplies the traditional first syllable for a name, the basis of the Namakaran
ceremony. The tool returns the syllable for your pada.

## What you get with it

Along with the nakshatra you get its gana, yoni and nadi. Those three classifications carry 18
of the 36 points in [kundali matching](/tools/kundali-milan/), so this is usually the first
calculation anyone runs before a match.
