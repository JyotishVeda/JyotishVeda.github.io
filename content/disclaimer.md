## Entertainment and education

All astrological content on JyotishVeda — rashi descriptions, nakshatra qualities, graha
significations, bhava meanings, rashifal readings, dosha assessments and matching scores — is
published for **education, cultural study and entertainment**.

Astrology is a traditional interpretive system with a long history in Indian culture. It has
not been demonstrated to predict events, personality or outcomes in controlled scientific
study, and nothing on this site should be understood as a claim that it does.

## What is measured and what is interpreted

The **astronomical positions** are calculated from an ephemeris and are verifiable. If a
sidereal longitude on this site is wrong, that is an error we want reported and will fix.

The **meanings** attached to those positions come from classical Jyotish literature. They are
presented as what the tradition holds, not as established fact. Where classical sources
disagree, we say so.

## Not professional advice

Nothing on this site is medical, psychological, legal, financial or relationship advice.

- **Health.** Do not delay, avoid or alter medical treatment on the basis of anything here.
  Medical Jyotish correspondences are traditional frameworks, not diagnostics. Consult a
  qualified doctor.
- **Money.** Do not make investment, business or financial decisions on astrological grounds.
  Consult a licensed financial adviser.
- **Legal.** Nothing here is legal advice.
- **Relationships.** Kundali matching scores must not be used to justify pressure, coercion or
  the prevention of a marriage that both people want. A koota score is not a measurement of
  anyone's compatibility or worth.

## Doshas and remedies

Mangal dosha, Sade Sati, Kaal Sarp and similar conditions are traditional classifications.
They are common in the population, they carry documented cancellations and exemptions, and
they are frequently used to sell expensive remedies.

We do not sell gemstones, pujas, yantras or remedial services, and we do not recommend any
provider. Please be cautious with anyone who responds to a dosha result by asking for money.

## Accuracy

Calculations follow standard published formulae and the Lahiri ayanamsha. Results may still
differ slightly from other software because of differing ayanamsha values, node conventions
(mean versus true) or year-length conventions in dasha arithmetic. No warranty of accuracy is
given, and the site is provided "as is".
