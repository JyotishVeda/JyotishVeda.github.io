## Why 120

Vimshottari means "one hundred and twenty". Nine grahas hold periods that sum to exactly that:

```
Ketu 7 + Shukra 20 + Surya 6 + Chandra 10 + Mangal 7
     + Rahu 18 + Guru 16 + Shani 19 + Budha 17 = 120
```

The order never changes. What changes between charts is where in the cycle you start.

## The starting point

The cycle opens with the lord of the nakshatra the Moon occupies at birth. Each graha rules
three of the 27 nakshatras, spaced nine apart, so the mapping is fixed: a Moon in Ashwini,
Magha or Mula opens with Ketu; in Rohini, Hasta or Shravana with Chandra; and so on.

## Balance at birth

The Moon will usually be part-way through its nakshatra at birth, and the tradition treats
that crossed portion as period already spent:

```
balance = (1 − fraction of nakshatra crossed) × lord's full years
```

Worked example. Suppose the Moon sits at 6°40′ into a nakshatra ruled by Shukra. The nakshatra
is 13°20′ wide, so exactly half has been crossed. Shukra's full period is 20 years, so the
balance at birth is 10 years — the Shukra mahadasha runs until age 10, and Surya's six-year
period begins there.

## Antardashas

Each mahadasha divides into nine antardashas in the same sequence, starting with the
mahadasha lord itself and proportioned by the same year counts:

```
antardasha years = (mahadasha years × antardasha lord's years) ÷ 120
```

So inside the 19-year Shani mahadasha, the Shani–Budha antardasha runs
`19 × 17 ÷ 120 = 2.69 years`, about two years and eight months. The subdivision continues to
pratyantardasha and beyond by the same rule.

## Year length

This site uses 365.2425 days per year, the Gregorian mean. Some traditional software uses
360-day "savana" years, which shifts long-range dates by a noticeable margin. If a dasha date
here differs from another program by weeks or months, the year convention is usually the
reason — check that before the ayanamsha.

## What a dasha does not do

Vimshottari answers *when*, not *what*. The results a period delivers depend on the graha's
house, sign, dignity, aspects and relationship to the lagna lord in that specific chart. The
period label on its own predicts nothing, and no dasha is inherently good or bad.
