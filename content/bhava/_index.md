Where a rashi is a division of the sky, a **bhava** is a division of life. The twelve bhavas
are counted from the lagna — the rashi rising on the eastern horizon at the moment of birth —
and each covers a defined set of concerns.

This site uses **whole-sign houses**, the standard method in Indian astrology: the rising
rashi is the entire 1st bhava, the next rashi is the entire 2nd, and so on. No cusp
arithmetic is involved, which also means no dispute about which house system to use.

Because the lagna moves through all twelve rashis every 24 hours — roughly one degree every
four minutes — an accurate birth time matters more for the bhavas than for anything else in
the chart.
