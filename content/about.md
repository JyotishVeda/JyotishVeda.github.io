JyotishVeda is an independent educational site about Jyotish — the classical Indian system of
astrology. It exists because most astrology sites hand you a verdict and hide the arithmetic.
This one shows the arithmetic.

## What the site does

Every calculator here computes real sidereal positions in your browser. Tropical longitudes
come from an astronomical ephemeris; the Lahiri (Chitrapaksha) ayanamsha is subtracted to give
sidereal values; rashis, nakshatras, padas, bhavas, dasha periods and panchang elements follow
from those numbers by the standard formulae.

Each result prints the intermediate values — the ayanamsha applied, the longitude used, the
fraction of a nakshatra crossed — so that anything on this site can be checked against another
ephemeris or another piece of software.

## The line we draw

The astronomy is measurable. The Moon's sidereal longitude at a given instant is a fact that
any ephemeris will confirm, and if this site got it wrong you could prove it.

The interpretation is not measurable. The meanings attached to those positions belong to a
traditional system with a long cultural history. They have not been demonstrated to predict
outcomes in controlled study, and this site does not claim otherwise. Where a classical rule
is disputed between schools, we say so rather than picking one silently.

That distinction is stated on every page for a reason. Astrology deserves to be presented
honestly — as a living cultural tradition worth understanding — rather than dressed up as
something it is not.

## Method notes

- **Ayanamsha:** Lahiri (Chitrapaksha), the Government of India standard
- **Houses:** whole-sign (Rashi), the standard Indian convention
- **Nodes:** Rahu and Ketu computed as the Moon's mean nodes
- **Dasha:** Vimshottari, 120-year cycle, 365.2425-day year
- **Simplifications:** the Vashya and Yoni kootas in Guna Milan are simplified, and each
  simplification is printed beside the score it affects
