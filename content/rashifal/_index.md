---

Most horoscope columns are written in advance by a person. This one is not written at all — it
is calculated. When the page loads, your browser works out where the Moon, Sun, Jupiter and
Saturn actually are in sidereal terms, counts the distance from the rashi you select, and
assembles the reading from the classical gochar rules for those transit positions.

The consequence is worth stating plainly: the reading changes because the sky changed, and the
same date always produces the same text. It also means the reading is only as relevant as the
rashi you choose — pick your **janma rashi** (the Moon's rashi at birth), not your Sun sign.
