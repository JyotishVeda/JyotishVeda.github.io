## Corrections come first

If you have checked a calculation against another ephemeris and found a discrepancy, please
tell us. Include the birth date, time, place, the value you got, the value this site returned
and the software you compared against. Calculation errors are treated as the highest priority
and are fixed before anything else.

## Get in touch

**Email:** mankeshwaracreations@gmail.com


We usually reply within a few working days. Messages are read by a person.

## What we can help with

- Errors in a calculation, a table or a classical reference
- Questions about the methods used — ayanamsha, house system, dasha conventions
- Requests for a topic to be covered in the Learn section
- Advertising and partnership enquiries
- Privacy questions and data requests

## What we cannot do

We do not offer personal readings, consultations or predictions, and we cannot interpret an
individual chart by email. The tools on this site are free and unlimited — use them as often
as you like.

We also cannot advise on medical, legal, financial or relationship decisions. Please consult a
qualified professional in the relevant field.
